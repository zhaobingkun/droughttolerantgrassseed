Warm-Season Comparison

Bermuda and zoysia are two of the best drought lawn options, but they solve the problem in different ways

This page matters because searchers often know they need a warm-season lawn, but they still do not know whether they want the faster, tougher bermuda path or the denser, calmer zoysia path.

Direct Answer

Choose bermuda for maximum heat toughness and recovery. Choose zoysia for a denser, steadier finish with strong drought logic.

Both belong in drought conversations. The real question is whether the yard values raw toughness or a more controlled lawn texture.

Why this comparison is valuable

Many homeowners are already past the cool-season debate by the time they reach this page. They are deciding between two warm-season answers that both look viable on paper.

Where bermuda wins

Bermuda usually wins when the lawn gets intense sun, the climate is hotter, and the yard needs tougher recovery and more aggressive performance.

Where zoysia wins

Zoysia often wins when the homeowner still wants warm-season drought strength but prefers a denser, steadier lawn personality instead of the harder-charging bermuda feel.
