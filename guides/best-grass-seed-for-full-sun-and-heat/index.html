Heat Guide

The best grass seed for full sun and heat usually comes from the warm-season side first

This page exists because many searchers are really describing the harshest lawn pattern: open exposure, heavy heat, and long summer stress. Once a lawn fits that pattern, the shortlist often becomes much clearer.

Direct Answer

For full sun and strong heat, warm-season grasses usually lead the shortlist.

The hotter and more exposed the yard gets, the more this page moves away from cool-season answers.

Why this page is different from a generic drought page

Some drought pages still have to consider mixed light, transition climates, or lower-intensity yards. This one is specifically about the lawns where strong sun and heat are already confirmed.

What the page should resolve

  • Whether the lawn clearly belongs in a warm-season shortlist
  • Whether the homeowner wants toughness, density, or lower input
  • Whether region-specific heat pages should come next
