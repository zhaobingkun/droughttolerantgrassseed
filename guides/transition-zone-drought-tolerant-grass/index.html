The transition zone is where drought tolerant grass decisions get hardest

Transition-zone pages are valuable because this is where homeowners most often struggle between summer survival and the kind of lawn they still want to look at for the rest of the year. It is the classic compromise zone.

Direct Answer

The transition zone usually rewards compromise, not an extreme answer.

Tall fescue often stays competitive here, but some yards still lean warm-season once summer pressure becomes too dominant.

Why the transition zone matters

This is where broad lawn advice breaks down fastest. The climate is hot enough to punish weak cool-season lawns, but many homeowners still resist a full warm-season switch.

What homeowners are really deciding

  • Whether they want a greener cool-season look enough to support it
  • Whether summer performance matters more than off-season appearance
  • How much irrigation they are actually willing to maintain
