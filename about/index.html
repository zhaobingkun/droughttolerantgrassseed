About this drought tolerant grass seed site

Drought Tolerant Grass Seed Guide is an independent reference site built around one practical topic: how homeowners can choose grass seed that survives dry periods better while still matching their climate, sunlight, and maintenance expectations.

The site is organized around the questions people search for most often: which seed is best, when to plant, which grass type fits the region, and what changes when a lawn must stay greener with less water.
