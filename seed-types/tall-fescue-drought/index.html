Seed Type

Tall fescue is often the best drought-aware answer when you still want a cool-season lawn

Tall fescue matters because plenty of homeowners do not want to give up the cool-season lawn look, even though they still need better summer resilience than weaker cool-season grasses often provide.

Direct Answer

Tall fescue is usually the best drought compromise for the transition zone.

It is not the pure warm-season drought champion, but it often wins when climate and appearance both matter.

Where tall fescue works best

Tall fescue earns its place in transition zones and yards where homeowners want a cool-season lawn that can still handle a tougher summer than bluegrass usually tolerates.

Why it keeps showing up in search

Many homeowners search drought tolerant grass seed but do not actually want a warm-season lawn. Tall fescue is often the page that resolves that tension.
