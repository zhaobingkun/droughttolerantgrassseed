Seed Type

Bermuda grass seed is often the strongest answer for hot, sunny drought lawns

Bermuda belongs on the shortlist when the yard gets strong sun, summer heat is serious, and the homeowner wants a durable lawn that can recover fast and tolerate dry stretches better once established.

Direct Answer

Choose bermuda when full sun and heat are the dominant conditions.

It is often the best low-water lawn option for southern climates that reward aggressive warm-season growth.

Best fit

Bermuda is strongest in hot regions and full-sun lawns. It is especially useful where summer performance matters more than winter color.

Why it keeps ranking for drought terms

Searchers who want the toughest warm-season lawn often end up on bermuda pages first. That happens because bermuda handles heat, sun, and recovery pressure better than many alternatives once it is established.

Main tradeoff

The same vigor that makes bermuda attractive can also make it feel aggressive. That is fine for many homeowners, but it is worth knowing before planting.

Where to look next

If bermuda seems close but not perfect, compare it with zoysia or review the Texas region page.
