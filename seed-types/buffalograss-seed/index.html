Seed Type

Buffalograss seed is a real drought option when low input matters more than a high-performance show lawn

Buffalograss belongs in this site because some homeowners do not want the most aggressive or the most manicured lawn. They want a lawn that survives heat and dry periods with less input and a more relaxed expectation set.

Direct Answer

Choose buffalograss when lower water use and lower input matter more than a dense, fast-recovering showcase lawn.

It is usually a fit for simpler, hotter, sunnier yards where expectations are realistic.

Why buffalograss deserves its own page

Buffalograss shows up in this keyword space because it represents a different homeowner goal. This is not always about the greenest or most durable lawn. Sometimes it is about maintaining acceptable turf with less water and fewer high-input habits.

Where it works best

Buffalograss is most useful where sun is abundant and the homeowner accepts a more natural, lower-input lawn character.

When it is not the first answer

If the lawn needs fast recovery, heavy traffic tolerance, or a tighter visual finish, bermuda or zoysia may stay ahead.
