Seed Type

Zoysia grass seed is a strong drought-tolerant option when you want density and a steadier lawn texture

Zoysia often appeals to homeowners who want warm-season drought strength but do not necessarily want the same feel or aggressiveness as bermuda. It sits well in lawns where long-term density matters.

Direct Answer

Choose zoysia when you want a drought-aware warm-season lawn with a denser, calmer growth habit.

It often works best for homeowners balancing appearance and water savings.

Where zoysia stands out

Zoysia can be attractive where bermuda feels too aggressive but warm-season drought performance still matters. It stays in the conversation for lawns that are as much about finish as toughness.

Where it may not be the first answer

If you want the fastest recovery and the hardest-charging heat lawn possible, bermuda may still be the simpler answer.
