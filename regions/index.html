Regional Hub

Drought tolerant grass seed choices change by region faster than broad national lists admit

Regional pages matter because dry inland California is not the same as the California coast, and Texas lawn expectations differ across North Texas, Central Texas, and the Gulf side. This hub starts with the strongest regional intent pages.

Takeaway

Use regional pages after you narrow your grass family.

Region pages are most helpful once you already know whether warm-season grass or tall fescue belongs in the conversation.
