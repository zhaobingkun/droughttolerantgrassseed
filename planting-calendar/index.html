Calendar Hub

Plant drought tolerant grass seed during the right temperature window, not just the first free weekend

The main calendar page exists because drought-tolerant lawns still fail if they are planted into heat stress, poor soil warmth, or the wrong shoulder season. Timing changes by grass family and region.

Core Rule

Warm-season seed wants warming soil. Tall fescue prefers fall in many transition zones.

The best time to plant depends on whether you are establishing bermuda or zoysia into rising summer warmth or tall fescue into cooler fall conditions.
